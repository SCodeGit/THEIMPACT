Declaration of Intent

I, FORGIVE EMERALD AFI MORNYUIE, a Level 200 student teacher offering Bachelor of Education in Junior High Education, together with my running mate Mr. ISRAEL KWADZO AGBETI, do hereby declare our intention to contest for the offices of SRC President and Vice President respectively for the 2026/2027 academic year at Peki College of Education.

"Leadership is service, and service is impact. We believe the SRC should not just represent students, it should transform their college experience."

That is why under the theme "THE IMPACT: Transforming Ideas Into Action", we present ourselves to lead, to listen, and to deliver.

As student teachers, we have been trained to understand people, communities, and systems. We have interacted with colleagues across levels and we understand our core challenges: accommodation, academics, welfare, sanitation, and student voice.

We are aspiring because we believe we can do more together. We bring the passion, humility, and commitment to turn student concerns into real, and measurable action.
